Mendo Tansuki was a fisherman. He would go into the ocean to fish, but one particular day, when he was striding along the coastline, he found a small old ring washed up on the shore. He was poor, so he kept it.As the days passed by, he tried several times to sell it, but it was such a priceless ring, nobody wanted to buy it. But one night, as he was strolling within his village in the bare moonlight, groups of raiders invaded the village. In fear, he began to flee, but then suddenly, he felt a strong spiritual energy surge in his body, which seemed to be channeled by the ring.When the sun rose the next day, all the raiders had already fallen to Mendo's newly found strength. He became a well-respected man for a few years, but then his power started to fade. He tried what he can to bring it back, but the ring didn't seem to work anymore.Angrily, he cast it back into the sea where it belongs.Many have tried to figure out what triggered the power of the ring, but no one has yet succeeded.A few years later, an anonymous  scroll was found. It seemed to be related to Mendo's ring. The writing on the scroll read:"To unravel the secret of the ancient relic, look into the celestial body that alters the tides on a clear night."
