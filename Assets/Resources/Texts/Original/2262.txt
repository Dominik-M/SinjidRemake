Sinjid left the arena, tired, but proud that he had finally fullfilled his master's will.But upon returning to his master's house, Fujin was gone. All that was left was a letter. It read:Sinjid,I have left the safe boundaries of the mountain to seek out an old friend, who lives far by the northern coastline. A Fallen Army, which was once driven away by our forces, has now regrouped and is starting to strike from the south. You are to defend the highlands. The tall trees here will provide excellent cover. At the misty peak of this mountain, I have arranged for the great one to meet with you. Seek him out.-Fujin
