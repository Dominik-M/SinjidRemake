Balanced fighters have a freedom of choice, but since they are 'balanced,' it will be difficult to focus on a primary attribute, which will make them weaker in the long run than attribute-based heroes.
Most skills have interlocking links to other skills, and attributes. Leveling up the skills that support each other can make you very powerful. Once you work out the right combination, the path to victory lays clear.
