The Dark Rift is the final and hardest portal in the temple. Enemies in there have special moves and attributes. Very few have managed to live through the battles, but those who returned told many things of what they had experienced while in there.Some say they faced a physically very powerful enemy, who seemed to be made of liquid metal, and dealt great damage with a single strike.Others came face to face with a strange spiritual being, which had a powerful, magical force field around it. Its attack is purely magic, and deals great damage.The other three enemies in there remain unknown, since the ones who survived them never told the outside anything about the nightmares they faced.
