Welcome to the temple. You are wise to come and read this guide. Those who ignore the power of knowledge will likely not last as long as the ones prepared.There are many ways to combat an enemy. Bash them with pure strength, blast them with mystical energy, charge at them with considerable speed...  the list goes on.First, you will need to know what each attribute does. Strength gives you more physical damage, and the power to carry stronger equipment. Speed will give you evasion and accuracy. The faster you are, the less chances you have of getting hit. If you are faster than the enemy, then you will get to attack first when a battle starts.Some enemies have items that you cannot buy. Once you defeat them, you will have a chance to obtain these items, but only if you have at least one inventory slot open. So it is wise to spare some space in your inventory everytime you enter a fight,The moves you should use also depends on the type of fighter you are. If you are a Warrior, then powerful physical attacks are the right choice. Spellcasters should use magic to vanquish their enemies. Shadow ninjas should utilize their great speed to a maximum extent. Balanced fighters have a freedom of choice, but since they are 'balanced,' it will be difficult to focus on a primary attribute, which will make them weaker in the long run than attribute-based heroes.Most skills have interlocking links to other skills, and attributes. Leveling up the skills that support each other can make you very powerful. Once you work out the right combination, the path to victory lays clear.
