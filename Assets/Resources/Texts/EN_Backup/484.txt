Fujin, though wounded emotionally by the loss of his friend, carefully ventures into the great mountains of Hejstu with Sinjid in his arms. Although there were many great and vile creatures lurking in the tall trees,  Hejstu was the safest place to be. No detectable settlement is safe from the Fallen Army, but they would not think of conquering the vast, empty mountains.Fujin establishes a small house there, and trains Sinjid, to combat the Fallen Army.After twenty years of solid training and meditation, Sinjid was ready to engage the Fallen, but he had not yet killed a person before, and so Fujin takes him to the great Shadow Temple, and instructs him to kill all the men in the Human Gateway that was there.Sinjid accepts this task, and so sets off to complete the final phase of his training, while Fujin sets off into misty peaks of the highest mountain, for a reason unknown to all but himself.
